The Castor Oil Rig Tales

I was commissioned to illustrate a fascinating and thrilling children's book about a community of organisms which colonize an abandoned oil rig, written by Sue Partenheimer. I illustrated one black-and-white portrait of each character and one larger colored illustration of a scene from each chapter.
