Science Comics

I was having a hard time staying focused and motivated while studying for my comprehensive exams, especially because I find it isolating to study alone. I thought it could be fun to start sharing these cartoons I've been drawing.

Captured topics include: 1. Genomic Conflict 2. Marine mammal comic / phocid vs otariid energetics 3. How to Win a Gal in 10 Days 4. Lessons in Democracy from the Animal Kingdom 5. To Share or Not to Share 6. Mentorship 7. Social Structure 10. Experimental Design

Recommendation: archive as a scrollable comics gallery with short science notes and optional alt text.
