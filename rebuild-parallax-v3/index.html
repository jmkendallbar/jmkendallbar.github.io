Sleep at Sea

As increasing human impact coincides with changing ecosystems, it is important to assess how our activities affect animals' natural behavior. Marine mammal sleep had only been studied in the lab. I combined art, engineering, and biology to develop a system to monitor and visualize the sleep patterns of seals at sea.
