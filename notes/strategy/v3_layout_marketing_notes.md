# Layout and marketing notes for v3

## Main hierarchy
1. Lead with Research + Publications and make EcoViz Lab impossible to miss.
2. Keep Sleep at Sea as the signature research story and visual identity.
3. Keep the personal portfolio as an archive of how research becomes public-facing science communication.
4. Retire store UX in favor of protected galleries, inquiry buttons, licensing, and commissions.

## Suggested public nav
Home · Research · Sleep at Sea · Teaching · Art · Gallery · Press · Bio · EcoViz Lab

## Why this works
The current site already has a compelling interdisciplinary identity: design, visualize, apply, communicate. The redesign keeps that structure but makes the research/lab/publications pathway more obvious and moves commerce out of the way.

## Gallery download deterrence
Use lower-resolution display assets, watermarks/overlays, CSS background images, disabled right-click/drag, and contact/licensing CTA. There is no real way to prevent downloads of images that are displayed publicly in a browser.
