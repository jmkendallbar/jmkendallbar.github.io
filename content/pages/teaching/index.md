# Teaching

## New Coursera Course
Data-Driven Animation for Science Communication.

My brand new course Data-Driven Animation for Science Communication was first taught as a UC-wide online course [UCSC SCIC110] in Spring 2023 and then brought to learners worldwide on Coursera in Fall 2023.

This course was created in collaboration with UCSC Online Education, UCSC Science Communication Program, CSUMB Science Illustration Program, and Burroughs Wellcome Fund.
