# Data Visualization
The convergence of art, science, and technology for digital storytelling.

## Explore our 3D virtual gallery
This quarter I led a data visualization collective that brought together undergraduate and graduate students from Art: Games and Playable Media, Computational Media, Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, and Astronomy and Astrophysics.

Students worked with UCSC scientists to create graphical abstracts with custom graphics, publication-ready figures coded in R/Python/JavaScript, 2D and 3D animations, interactive websites, RShiny apps, ObservableHQ notebooks, and final projects combining course concepts.

## Final projects / student gallery
The gallery includes descriptions of each student’s project, the skills they learned, and links to websites/source code. Projects include hydrodynamical simulations of stars and black holes, human impacts on trophic activity patterns, bycatch in fisheries, Scott Creek fish passage, elephant seal census data, California ocean temperature projections, fur seal and sea lion isotopes, cryptic female choice simulations, drought effects on aquatic invertebrates, spring run Chinook salmon graphical abstracts, and Lightscapes of Fear.
