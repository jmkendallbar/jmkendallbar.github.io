# Videos

## Animations
Explore animations created in Adobe Animate, After Effects, Autodesk Maya, and MATLAB.

### Skinny seals sacrifice safety for sustenance
Animation by Jessica Kendall-Bar. Elephant seal illustrations by Danielle Dube through an Art-Science Residency with the Norris Center for Natural History. Music by Connor Vance.

### Visualizing Life in the Deep
Video abstract for the manuscript "Visualizing Life in the Deep: A Creative Pipeline for Data-Driven Animations to Facilitate Marine Mammal Research, Outreach, and Conservation." The workflow turns animal-borne tag data into animations and sound to facilitate research, communication, and conservation.

### The Value of US Coral Reefs for Flood Risk Reduction in Nature Sustainability
Coral reefs in the US prevent flooding to 53,833 people, economic damages by $5.3 billion, and additional flooding of 113 km2 for the 100-year storm. If 1 m of reefs are lost, floodplains and damages increase substantially.

### Selkie Dreams Animation
A surreal underwater dream animation about fur seal sleep, unihemispheric sleep, eye state, and the watery boundary between science and myth.
